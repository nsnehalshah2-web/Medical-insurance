        Under the hood

        How the model works

        How the model was chosen and what it learned. The full analysis is in the project notebook.

        1. 1

          Explore

          1,338 records (1 duplicate removed). Charges are right-skewed, and smokers pay about 3.8× more.

        2. 2

          Engineer

          BMI × smoker and age × smoker interactions, plus WHO BMI bands.

        3. 3

          Compare

          Four algorithms on base and engineered features, with 5-fold cross-validation.

        4. 4

          Tune

          Grid search over Gradient Boosting, on a log-transformed target.

        5. 5

          Test

          A held-out 20% of people, never seen during training or tuning.
